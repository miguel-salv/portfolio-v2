## Authoring

`build_hero.py` reuses the existing matcher exhibit builder before its presentation-unit normalization. It preserves 1:1 metric CAD geometry and the existing photo-referenced capacitor/inductor/RF harness reconstruction. The source `.blend` and production assets are untouched. The large salvaged capacitor's plate outline/count and RF wire routing remain reconstruction estimates, as documented by the existing builder.

The room and RF bench retain `world.json` dimensions. The 35 mm lens, +5° hardware yaw and southeast heading come from the corrected motion study. The opening camera is moved closer along that same view, and the mug sits just behind the notebook. The portrait uses the same viewing direction, a translated framing, 42 mm lens and shifted film gate; hardware geometry stays fixed. The notebook moves closer to the matcher in the shared scene so its chart survives the phone crop. The green mat extends toward the bench back, and the floor is dark graphite, providing a physical quiet area for text in the window light. The notebook contains mathematical constant-resistance/reactance loci, with an explicitly illustrative convergence trace.

The source now shares the film's rear-panel coax connector and supported cable route. The opening PNG/WebP stills predate that cable correction and were not rendered again during the Blender scrub pass.

Rendering uses physical materials, fixed west-window sky fill, a low morning key passing through the modeled window, AgX, denoising, and optical blur that keeps the hardware readable. Oak is the photographed [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01) material by Jenelle van Heerden, licensed CC0; official diffuse, roughness, normal and height files are checksum-verified and packed into the Blender scene. Other small materials are authored procedurally. `hero/render.json` records final render settings, source fingerprint, source bounds and camera projections.


The small authoring source, preview and notes are versionable. `.blend`, PNG/WebP frames and render reports stay in the ignored local `hero/` directory.
