# Vehicle frame evidence and reconstruction

Updated 2026-10-04. `model.py` reconstructs the visible mechanical frame from the project photograph; it does not import a verified original chassis CAD file.

## Evidence used

- **Primary geometry reference:** `src/assets/vehicle-cover.jpg`. This is the actual hardware: thin black upper sheet, green formed lower tray, mild front taper, transverse front aperture, central service apertures, groups of circular mounting holes, brass standoffs, black PCB posts, sweeping tire grooves, ten turbine-style black spokes, red outer ring with inward fingers, green front steering supports and three upward-facing bumper screws.
- **Custom electronics reference:** `src/assets/vehicle-pcb.jpg`. The existing custom control PCB, electronics arrangement, connectors and black harness remain in the model.
- **Manufacturer product reference:** [Yahboom ROS-Robot-Chassis](https://github.com/YahboomTechnology/ROS-Robot-Chassis), especially [Ros_Robot_Chassis.jpg](https://github.com/YahboomTechnology/ROS-Robot-Chassis/blob/main/Ros_Robot_Chassis.jpg). The lower-right Ackermann product strongly resembles the photographed frame: green tray, black deck, red touring rims, steering axle, brass supports and three-screw foam bumper. This is a visual match, not a confirmed purchase/model identity.
- **Official chassis documentation:** [ROS Robot Chassis](https://www.yahboom.net/study/ROS-chassis). Manufacturer describes a green aluminum-alloy frame and 520 encoder motors in its [README](https://github.com/YahboomTechnology/ROS-Robot-Chassis/blob/main/README.md). This supports separating the green structural metal material from the custom PCB material.
- **Related mechanical architecture:** [ROSMASTER R2 manufacturer description](https://category.yahboom.net/blogs/news/unboxing-and-review-rosmaster-r2) and [R2 documentation](https://www.yahboom.net/study/ROSMASTER-R2). These identify Ackermann front steering with two reduction motors and road tires. They are architecture references, not a dimensional identification of this project’s chassis.

## Exact CAD investigation

The official chassis page points to [Chassis 3D model file](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1P-BAtq7WcaXdJArmkcGqBwj6-o_dBms9). On the inspection date it exposed one 41.1 MB `Chassis 3D model file.zip` archive. The inspected archive contains the regular `4WD chassis without suspension chassis` STEP files (common wheel and Mecanum variants) plus dimension images, rather than the matching Ackermann frame. The GitHub repository contains installation links, driving tutorials and motor parameters; its recursive file listing contains no Ackermann STEP, STL or other chassis mesh. The related R2 hardware annex contains electronics information; no matching frame CAD was exposed there. The old chassis store URL currently returns 404.

The unrelated 4WD STEP is therefore **not imported**. A different wheelbase or sheet-hole pattern should not be represented as an exact match merely because its lower plate is green.

## Geometry confidence

**Strong visual confidence:** thin plate construction, broader front of the black deck, rounded/clipped deck corners, real holes and service openings, red rim width, ten tapered/swept spokes, recessed curved tread, one continuous foam bumper, screw orientation and relative positions, brass columns, front wheel-support ears and separate structural green material.

**Estimated from perspective:** exact plate outline coordinates, hole centers/pitch, sheet thickness, tire cross section, bumper dimensions and recess depths, formed tray rail height, and internal mount spacing. The established renderer’s overall envelope and tire radius are preserved; scene units are not advertised as surveyed millimeters.

**Simplified/partly obscured construction:** rear motor saddles and encoder covers, battery retention, steering servo, kingpin blocks, cross tie rod, ball joints and colored servo cable routing. These follow the visible hardware and manufacturer architecture, but hidden joints and exact mechanical clearances were not measured. The static tie rod is illustrative; it is not a constraint-solved linkage simulation.

## Render integration

`build_vehicle()` and `animate_vehicle()` retain their call signatures, root name, four steering and rolling parents, wheelbase/track and 0.75 rolling radius. The shared story renderer can build the refined model without a mesh-import dependency. It continues seating the assembled object on the ground before animation.

`vehicle-studio.blend` is the original source snapshot and has not been overwritten. New catalogue previews live under `.impeccable/renders/model-refinement/vehicle/`; no public animations are generated by the model builder.
