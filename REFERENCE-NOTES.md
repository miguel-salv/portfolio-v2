# Portfolio reference study

The shortlist came from [Awwwards’ developer collection](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/developer/). The actual websites were opened, scrolled, and inspected. The selection deliberately spans an editorial designer portfolio, a physical product company, a motion studio, and a hardware presentation. Subject matter alone does not determine a useful reference.

One composition governs the result: Gil Huybrecht. Seasats and Warm & Fuzzy contribute two specific disciplines within that composition. USAvionix is a comparison, not a fourth visual language. All portfolio images, models, project facts, career information, and identity remain Miguel’s.

## Primary: Gil Huybrecht

[Homepage](https://gilhuybrecht.com/) · [Profile](https://gilhuybrecht.com/profile) · [Awwwards entry](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gil-huybrecht)

- **Composition and type:** The homepage places compact information in aligned columns above an image contact sheet. The profile uses an unusually large, plain grotesque paragraph, with an indented beginning and very little supporting decoration. The portfolio reproduces the hierarchy: a broad Archivo headline, a small factual introduction, a shared outer grid, and compact navigation. About gives the existing engineering biography a large paragraph opposite the portrait. Archivo is self-hosted; it serves both display and reading text, with the existing mono restricted to measurements.
- **Projects and images:** His image titles and sequence numbers sit above rectangular images, outside their boundaries. The new three-column engineering index closely reproduces that caption/image relationship using the actual matcher, vehicle, and robot build photographs. Each image has the same 4:3 aperture, the same top baseline, a title above, and project context below. At 1440px, the shared outer gutter is 52px and column gap is 24px. On a phone the images stack in the same reading order.
- **Navigation and transitions:** Compact native labels and restrained active treatment carry wayfinding. Miguel’s header keeps its existing links, search, theme switch and mobile menu. The chapter rail uses a clear blue current label and underline. The three engineering cards retain the existing 560ms image-to-case-study FLIP transition, including their current crops and white balance. Keyboard activation follows the same transition; reduced motion and modified clicks retain ordinary navigation. Gil’s alternate Star Wars presentation is not part of the portfolio direction.
- **Hover and focus:** Image emphasis remains a small, bounded enlargement. Text links use underlines or color, with a visible keyboard outline. Cards stay real links; wake and chase controls remain separate buttons. The active chapter has a persistent treatment rather than depending on hover.
- **Pacing:** An image-dense catalogue follows the quiet hero; the interactive mechanism occupies a larger space; the two playful builds form another compact gallery; the charcoal biography and flat career disclosures conclude the story. This makes the varied work read as one edited collection.
